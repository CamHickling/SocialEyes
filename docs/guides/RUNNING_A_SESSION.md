# Running a session

A one-page checklist for whoever runs participants. Print it and keep it by the phone.
Words in **bold** are buttons or screens in the SocialEyes app (phone) or desktop app (computer).

## The day before

- [ ] On the computer, open the study and choose **Phone**, then **Load onto phone**. Fix anything
      marked in red; yellow warnings (battery, Do Not Disturb) can wait until the day.
- [ ] Charge the phone (and the Neon glasses and their phone, if the study uses eye tracking).
- [ ] Have enough consent forms, and a run sheet to note each participant's id and anything unusual.

## Setting up (each day)

- [ ] Phone: **Do Not Disturb on**, screen clean, not facing a window or a bright lamp.
- [ ] Open **SocialEyes** and tap the study. Check the battery and storage lines aren't red.
- [ ] Decide once whether to **Pin the app during sessions** (the switch stays as you leave it).
      Pinned, a participant can't leave the app by accident; the phone may ask for its PIN after
      each session.
- [ ] Eye tracking: glasses plugged in, Neon Companion open, the **SocialEyes** wearer profile chosen.

## For each participant

1. Consent first (your lab's forms). Never write the participant's name anywhere in SocialEyes.
2. Tap **Start the next participant: P0xx** and write that id on the run sheet. Don't reuse an id;
   the app asks if you pick one that has been used.
3. Eye tracking: on the **eye tracker check**, tick each item and tap **Start recording**. If the
   glasses won't connect and the study allows it, **Continue without eye tracking** and note it.
4. If pinning is on, tap **Got it** on Android's pinning message.
5. Hand over the phone; give the instructions your protocol says. Stay out of view of the screen.
6. When the phone shows **Thank you!**, take it back. Note anything unusual (interruptions, the
   glasses slipping, a question asked) on the run sheet.

**If something goes wrong**

| What happened | What to do |
|---|---|
| The participant wants to stop | Press **Back**, then **Stop session**. What was recorded is kept and marked as stopped. |
| The participant withdraws, now or later | Note it. On the computer: **Data**, the participant's row, **Withdraw…**. Their data is set aside, can be restored if it was a mistake, and is deleted only with **Delete permanently…**. |
| The app closed or the phone restarted | Open SocialEyes again and start a new session with a **new** id; the interrupted one is kept. |
| The glasses disconnected mid-session | Carry on; the session notes it. Reconnect them before the next participant. |
| A call or notification came in | Carry on; the session records it. Check Do Not Disturb is on. |

## End of the day

- [ ] Connect the phone to the computer. Open the study's **Data** page and choose **Copy from the
      phone**. Every session should say *copied and checked*.
- [ ] Add the run sheet's notes to the **Notes** column of `sessions.csv` (open it in Excel; the app
      keeps your notes).
- [ ] The phone keeps its sessions until you choose **Clear copied from the phone**. Do that when the
      phone gets full or at the end of the study: it deletes only sessions whose copy on the
      computer (and second copy) still matches.
- [ ] Charge the phone and the glasses.
